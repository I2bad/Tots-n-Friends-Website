Is my child on track?

A short check you can do at home. It takes about five minutes. Use it with the progress report we send you.

A guide, not a test. It cannot diagnose anything.

Start the check Read your report

What the words in your child’s report mean

Every child learns at their own speed. Our report shows where your child is right now. Here is what each level means.

Exploring

Just starting

Your child is new to this skill and needs a lot of help. They may need a grown-up to show them how. They try, but it is hard.

Growing

Getting there

Your child can do it sometimes, with a little help. It goes well some days and not others. This is the normal middle.

Confident

Does it alone

Your child does it on their own, most of the time. They may even show a friend how.

Most children are Growing in most areas, most of the time. That is normal. A child can be Exploring in one area and Confident in another.

Check how your child is growing

This check is for toddlers aged 2 to 3 years. Think about the last two or three weeks. Pick the answer that is true on most days.

  1. Answer 18 short questions
  2. See your child’s picture
  3. Try one idea at home

We do not save your answers. Close the page and they are gone. Checks for other ages are coming. Until then, ask your child’s teacher.

Talking and listening

Words, sentences and following simple requests

Does your child put two or more words together, like “more milk”?
Does your child point to body parts when you ask, like “Where is your nose?”
Can your child follow a simple request, like “Bring me your shoes”?

Feelings and friends

Sharing, calming down and joining in

Does your child notice when someone is hurt or upset?
Does your child copy what other children and grown-ups do?
Does your child play next to other children, even if not yet with them?

Thinking and learning

Curiosity, pretend play, sorting and remembering

Does your child play pretend, like feeding a doll or talking on a toy phone?
Does your child sort or match things, like putting all the cars together?
Does your child look for a toy you hide while they watch?

Big body skills

Running, kicking, climbing and balance

Does your child kick a ball?
Does your child run without falling every few steps?
Does your child walk up steps, holding on if they need to?

Small hand skills

Spoons, blocks and crayons

Does your child scoop food with a spoon?
Does your child stack four or more blocks?
Does your child scribble with a crayon?

Looking after myself

Eating, dressing and toilet habits

Does your child try to help when you dress them, like pushing an arm through a sleeve?
Does your child drink from an open cup?
Does your child show you when a nappy is wet or dirty, or tell you they need the toilet?

Your child’s picture

Each colour is one area. A fuller arc means more answers were “Most of the time”.

Look at the whole picture. One area that needs time does not mean your child is behind. A child who is Growing in five areas and Exploring in one is a very normal child.

Let’s have a chat

Two or more areas could use some support. Please talk to your child’s teacher. We can show you what we see in class and plan next steps together. If you are worried, your child’s doctor or the nearest government health clinic can check too.

This check is a guide. It is not a medical test or a diagnosis. It cannot replace a visit to your doctor or a child health clinic. If you are worried about your child’s health or development, please see a health professional.

When to talk to us

You do not need a bad result to ask. If something feels off, say so. You know your child best, and we would rather hear it early.

  • Your child has lost skills they used to have, like words they used to say.
  • Your child does not turn when you call their name.
  • Two or more areas show “Let’s talk”.
  • You simply have a worry.
