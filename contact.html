Questions parents ask us

What ages do you accept?

Children from 6 months old up to 8 years. We have infant care (from 6 months), a toddler class (1 to 3 years), preschool / tadika (3 to 6 years), and The Creative Hive, our Thursday enrichment class.

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. We are closed on Saturdays, Sundays and Malaysian public holidays.

Where are you located?

No. 17, Jalan Jambu Mawar 3, Taman Sri Jarom, 42600 Jenjarom, Selangor, Malaysia. We serve families in Jenjarom and nearby parts of Selangor.

How do I enrol my child?

Message us on WhatsApp, call, or use the form on this page. We'll book you a tour, explain the programmes, and help with enrolment when you're ready.

What curriculum or teaching approach do you use?

We draw on Reggio Emilia and Montessori ideas. In practice, children learn through play, hands-on activities and good relationships, and their curiosity sets the direction.

What languages are used at the centre?

We teach and communicate in English, giving children a strong foundation in the language from an early age.

How can I contact you?

WhatsApp or call +60 17-307 8621, email janet@tpi.edu.my, or use the form on this page. We reply within one business day.
